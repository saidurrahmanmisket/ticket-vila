Protecting your privacy: Our Commitment to you

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 20.02.2024

Welcome to Ticket Villa, your trusted partner in House raffle. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to safeguard your data.

1. Information Collection

Personal Information:

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including but not limited to your name, email address, postal address, and telephone number. This information is collected when you register on our site, subscribe to our newsletter, participate in a raffle, or engage in other activities or services we provide.

Non-Personal Information:

We also collect non-personal information about your visit, including browsing history, IP address, and the type of device used. This data is gathered through cookies and similar technologies to enhance your user experience.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process transactions and deliver services.
  • Improve customer service and respond to inquiries.
  • Personalize your experience and cater to your individual needs.
  • Send periodic emails regarding updates, promotions, and relevant news, from which you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Information Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. These include encryption, firewalls, and secure server hosting. Your personal information is only accessible by a limited number of employees who have special access rights and are required to keep the information confidential.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties, except when necessary to provide a service or conduct our business operations as described in this policy. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for future processing.
  • Express concerns regarding your data's processing.

6. Third-Party Links

Occasionally, at our discretion, we may include or offer third-party products or services on our website. These third-party sites have separate and independent privacy policies. We, therefore, have no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites.

7. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review our Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting the personal information we collect.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@ticketvilla.com By using our site, you consent to our website's privacy policy.
